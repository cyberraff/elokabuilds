## 2. ABOUT SECTION → Rename to "Our Approach" or "Why Eloka Builds"

### Rewrite
> **Heading:** Why Eloka Builds
>
> Most agencies ask you to pay first and hope for the best. We flipped that.
>
> Eloka Builds is built around one idea: **show the work before asking for the money.** Before we ever reach out to a business, we've already built them a real, working website — fully designed, fully functional, ready to publish. You're not buying a promise or a mockup. You're looking at the finished product.
>
> Behind Eloka Builds is Raphael Ejeogo, a web developer who got tired of watching good local businesses lose customers to outdated, slow websites — and tired of watching agencies charge upfront for work clients hadn't seen yet. So the model changed: build first, prove it, then get paid.
>
> No account managers. No lengthy contracts. No surprises. Just a finished site and a straightforward yes or no.

**Why this fixes it:** Opens zoomed-out ("Eloka Builds is built around...") rather than "I'm Raphael, I do X, Y, Z" — reads as a studio with a philosophy, not a bio page. Your personal story is still there (this is your credibility asset — don't lose it), but it's framed as *why the company exists*, not as an about-me blurb. Drop the "when I'm not building websites..." hobby line entirely for the agency version — save that kind of personal color for a LinkedIn bio or a dedicated team/founder page later.

## 3. CASE STUDIES — Fix the "Placeholder" trust leak

Currently live copy: *"Placeholder data from past portfolio — will be replaced with Silent Contractor client work as deals close."* This tells prospects you have zero live clients under this model. Remove it and reframe instead:

### Section intro (replace placeholder disclaimer)
> **Heading:** Recent Work
>
> A look at recent builds and redesigns. Every project below was designed and developed end-to-end by our team.

### For each case study card, if it predates Eloka Builds / Silent Contractor:
Instead of flagging it as placeholder, reframe honestly but confidently — e.g.:
> "Website redesign for [Industry] business — full rebuild focused on speed and mobile conversion."

Drop industry-specific claims you can't back (fake metrics, fake client names) — but there's no need to tell visitors it's "not real Silent Contractor work." Let the work speak; if asked directly, you can always clarify in conversation or a Loom.

**If you have zero presentable work yet:** rename the section "What We Build" and show 2–3 of your demo sites framed as *capability examples* rather than "case studies" (case studies implies paid client outcomes; capability examples don't). This is honest and still impressive.
